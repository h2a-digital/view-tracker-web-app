# Tracking and New York accounting upgrade

Apply `supabase/migrations/202611080001_tracking_and_new_york.sql` manually to the existing project, once, as postgres. Stop the old application/worker first. The original migration is unchanged. No migration or worker was run against the live database during this implementation.

The incremental transaction retains all account IDs/provider links, posts and snapshots. It adds optional tracking and structured review evidence, detailed synchronization diagnostics, and an explicit `America/New_York` statement timezone. Finalized financial rows remain immutable. Only nonfinancial tracking metadata may refresh on finalized posts. The migration increments the optimistic revision and invalidates draft bonus approvals. If existing statements would gain/lose posts when changing Rome months to New York, or the new month would merge conflicting policies, it aborts before changing anything. That case needs a reviewed transition plan; do not bypass the guard or delete historical statements.

Publication instants stay UTC; deadlines remain elapsed `windowDays × 24 hours`, including across daylight saving transitions. Monthly pools, caps, rule-effective month boundaries, reporting, exports and database guards now use New York. Manual date fields require an ISO timestamp with an explicit offset or Z. The provider adapter validates timestamp strings before converting them.

The worker should still be invoked every five minutes and will enqueue connected accounts approximately hourly. New posts are routed through date-based campaign assignments. Previously discovered posts are not repeatedly inserted/audited. Analytics queries overlap the last successful account sync by two days and include any older still-open windows; an initial sync can backfill up to the provider's 365-day history budget. A single worker execution reuses its analytics response instead of calling it twice.

Samples are daily during ordinary tracking and can be hourly in the final six hours. Changed in-window counters also update a clearly provisional planning estimate without forcing another historical snapshot. At the deadline, a reading whose provider timestamp is before the deadline does not complete tracking. Hourly follow-ups continue up to 24 elapsed hours; missing timestamps, missing counters and failed refreshes remain visible. Fresh closing data must have a provider timestamp at or after the deadline and no more than two hours behind collection. Duplicate cached closing observations are skipped. Historical posts discovered after the grace period receive a late diagnostic reading and an explicit missing-history flag.

A fresh post-deadline reading completes **collection**, not verified eligibility. Its additional views are never added to provisional seven-day totals or automatically made payable. Exact cutoff uncertainty remains until evidenced. The grace period does not extend the 168-hour earning window. Public counters cannot independently verify organic-only traffic, artificial views, or the exact traffic allocation between polls. Do not interpolate cached readings into authoritative payout totals.

Every run records fetched posts, new posts, received/matched counters, snapshots saved, changed provider readings, fresh/stale/missing counts, skip-reason totals, errors, and up to 200 per-post observation outcomes. `success: true` means processing completed, while these diagnostics explain freshness/coverage. Temporary failures use the existing leased queue and exponential retries. Revoked credentials require reconnection. An expired closing grace period becomes a review exception instead of unbounded per-post polling.

Draft statements are generated automatically and provisional estimates are computed using observed in-window public counts only. Fixed payments, progressive CPM, manual bonus approval and the $3,000 cap are preserved. Payable amounts still require accepted eligibility; final payouts and discretionary bonuses remain manually approved. Review requires an auditable reference, explicit organic/cutoff confirmation and exactly matching source-window start/end. Corrections retain their evidence separately. The administrator is responsible for the authenticity of the cited export; the application cannot verify the contents of external documents.

Needs attention groups posts needing attribution, missing/stale/failed data, historical coverage, cutoff review or organic verification, and exposes run diagnostics. Active posts with current counters stay out of the organic-only review queue until the window closes. Because Zernio's public counts do not verify organic attribution, normal TikTok posts will still need an organic evidence decision before payment. This limitation cannot be safely eliminated by treating API success as verification.

Deployment checklist: apply the incremental migration; restart/deploy the updated app; resume the existing five-minute Cron caller with the same worker URL and secret; verify a newly published TikTok video and repeated counters; inspect the first closing cycle and evidence review before onboarding paid creators. The database does not schedule itself, and a sleeping local laptop or paused/free hosted service cannot collect unattended.
